## Formula Checklist

- Use the null-balance principle: no current through the measured branch at
  balance.
- Calibrate the potentiometer working current before measuring unknown emf.
- Convert scale/balance readings to emf according to the current template.
- For thermocouple tasks, analyze emf versus temperature and report slope or
  trend when required.

## Common Pitfalls

- Measuring emf above the working voltage without acknowledging the limitation.
- Treating potentiometer as an ordinary voltmeter instead of a null method.
- Skipping calibration before unknown emf measurement.
