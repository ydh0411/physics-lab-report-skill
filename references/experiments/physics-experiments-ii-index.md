## Physics II Template Notes

- Physics II may use prelab, short lab report, or long lab report templates.
- Old reports may use Abstract worth 3 or 10 points. Always read the current
  cover/template and section point values.
- Teacher initials/signatures appear on data sheets; attach completed scans
  rather than generating signatures.
