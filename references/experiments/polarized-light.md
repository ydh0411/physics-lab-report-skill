# Polarized Light

## Aliases

- Polarized Light
- Polarization of Light
- Polarization
- Malus' Law
- 偏振光
- Lab 5 current label, Experiment 7 historical label

## Use This Card When

The report verifies Malus' law and studies linear, elliptical, circular
polarization using polarizers, a quarter-wave plate, and a half-wave plate.

## Required Data Tables

- Data Table 7-1: Malus-law photocurrent versus analyzer angle, including
  initial polarizer/analyzer positions.
- Data Table 7-2: quarter-wave plate positions, maximum/minimum intensities,
  and polarization state.
- Data Table 7-3: half-wave plate rotation, extinction positions, position
  shifts, and theoretical `2 alpha` comparison.

## Formula And Figure Checklist

- Malus' law: `I = I0 cos^2(theta)`.
- Linear fit: `I = a cos^2(theta) + b`, with slope, intercept, and fit quality.
- Half-wave plate: output polarization rotates by `2 alpha`.
- Quarter-wave plate: identify linear, elliptical, and circular cases from
  intensity maxima/minima.
- Generate vector figures for `I` versus `theta` and/or `I` versus `cos^2(theta)`
  when required by the report.

## Report Pattern

- Abstract: summarize Malus-law verification plus wave-plate behavior.
- Calculations: sample Malus-law substitutions, linear regression, half-wave
  shift examples.
- Comments: discuss background current, non-ideal polarizers, angular
  resolution, detector linearity, and extinction ratio.
- Questions: connect polarization to transverse waves and distinguish
  unpolarized from circularly polarized light using a quarter-wave plate and
  analyzer.

## Common Pitfalls

- Treating non-zero current near extinction as fabrication or failure; it may be
  background current or imperfect extinction.
- Forgetting that `theta` is relative to the analyzer/polarizer reference
  position.
- Reporting circular polarization without checking max/min equality.
